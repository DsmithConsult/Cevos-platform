# CEVOS Code of Conduct

## Our Commitment

The CEVOS community is dedicated to providing a welcoming, inclusive, and respectful environment for everyone, regardless of experience level, background, profession, nationality, physical appearance, disability, or identity.

We believe the best engineering solutions emerge from open collaboration, constructive discussion, and mutual respect.

By participating in this project, contributors, maintainers, and community members agree to uphold the standards outlined in this Code of Conduct.

---

## Our Values

CEVOS is built on four foundational principles:

### 1. Respect

Treat all community members with professionalism and courtesy.

Respect differing technical opinions and perspectives.

### 2. Collaboration

Work together toward shared goals.

Encourage knowledge sharing and community learning.

### 3. Transparency

Communicate clearly and honestly about project decisions, changes, and limitations.

Share information in a way that helps the broader community participate meaningfully.

### 4. Inclusivity

Create space for contributors from different backgrounds and skill levels.

Support newcomers and help make participation accessible and welcoming.

---

## Expected Behavior

Community members are expected to:

- Be kind, patient, and constructive
- Listen actively and respond respectfully
- Give and receive feedback professionally
- Help create a safe and welcoming environment
- Focus on the technical and community value of ideas

---

## Unacceptable Behavior

The following behaviors are not acceptable:

- Harassment, discrimination, or personal attacks
- Trolling, insulting, or disruptive conduct
- Sharing private or sensitive information without permission
- Repeatedly dismissing others' contributions unfairly
- Any behavior that undermines a respectful and inclusive community

---

## Enforcement

Maintainers may take appropriate action in response to unacceptable behavior, including:

- Private warning
- Temporary moderation action
- Removal from discussion spaces or contribution activities
- Permanent exclusion from the project community when necessary

---

## Reporting Concerns

If you experience or witness behavior that violates this Code of Conduct, please contact the project maintainers through the repository's appropriate communication channel or reporting method.

Reports will be handled with care, respect, and confidentiality as appropriate.
